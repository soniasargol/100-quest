Your Toolkit
vs. Procrastination — Quicksand

Build the muscle on tiny stuff

Some days the win is just picking up the guitar for 60 seconds. That's enough. Momentum starts smaller than you think.

vs. Self-doubt — Underdog

Compare to day-1 you, not a guitarist

You have zero background — that's the whole point of the story. Practice hungry for your own respect, not applause.

vs. Not enough time — Eleventh hour

Lower the bar, just do 5 minutes

A messy 5-minute shitty-first-draft session still counts as a logged day. Get excited by the challenge, not the clock.

vs. Distraction — Siren song

Go full Ulysses

Leave the guitar out in plain sight, put your phone in another room before you sit down. Remove the choice entirely.

vs. Perfectionism — Treasure

The roughest of rough maps is fine

A rough, recognizable version of each song by day 100 is the actual goal. Big treasure — one perfect performance — is a lie.
